## The open application layer for personal AI

Eigin is a personal AI agent built to be private, safe and under your control.

Your agent, memory and tools run on your device. Choose your model, shape your agent and decide what it can do.

- [Explore Eigin](https://eigin.ai/en)
- [Read the documentation](https://docs.eigin.ai)
- [Explore our repositories](https://github.com/eigin)
- [Join the conversation](https://chat.eigin.ai)
